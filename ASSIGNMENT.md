## Project: Local AI Experiment

Document your experience running and comparing a local AI model.

### Requirements
- Install and run **Ollama** or **LM Studio** (see [INSTRUCTIONS-LOCAL-AI.md](INSTRUCTIONS-LOCAL-AI.md))
- Complete the **Three Prompts Exercise**: give the same three prompts to a local model and a cloud model (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini)
  - Prompt 1: treat the AI as a **tool** — ask it to do something for you
  - Prompt 2: treat the AI as a **collaborator** — work on something together
  - Prompt 3: treat the AI as a **subject** — ask it about itself
- Include any **creative artifact** generated during the experiment (a text file, exported conversation, or single-page HTML)

Your observations go directly in the **Local AI Log** section of your reflection — no separate notes file needed.

## Reflection

Write a **~300-word reflection** using the [reflection template](reflection-template.md).

The template has **5 sections** this week:
1. What I Made
2. **Local AI Log** — fill in the five questions about your experiment
3. Process & Decisions
4. Critical Prompt Response
5. Personal Connection

**This week's critical prompt (Part 4):**

> *"What changes when you run an AI on your own computer vs. accessing one through a browser? Who controls the tool — and does that change how you use it or trust it?"*

Think about: data privacy, internet access, speed, model size, who decides what the model can say, and how your relationship to the tool felt different (or the same).
